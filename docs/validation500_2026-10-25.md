# Frozen canonical method: 500-point validation

The final **Flash v2 + DINOv2 + supervised mixture + low-safety expert** was
fitted on 488 development points and frozen before scoring the 500 validation
points. Predictions were sealed before the validation reference was compared.
The exact same fitted artifacts subsequently scored the remaining 12,160 points;
there was no post-validation retraining or parameter selection.

## Population and correspondence

- All 500 IDs and image pairs match the reference metadata in canonical order:
  A = selected 0/90-degree image; B = selected 180/270-degree image.
- 405 reference points have all 12 numeric targets: **4,860 evaluable items**.
  The other 95 points have all-NA reference scores (1,140 items) and are excluded
  from error metrics. They remain in the 500-row prediction delivery.
- No reference-guided side swaps are included in the metrics below.
- Development and validation point IDs are disjoint. An image-byte audit found
  four image hashes shared with development at two validation points. All 500
  were retained as requested. Thus this is the planned held-out point-list
  evaluation, with a known exact-image overlap caveat, not perfectly image-disjoint
  evidence. No overlap-excluded sensitivity estimate is reported here.

## All-target results

Within-0.1 means the observed proportion of numeric score items with absolute
error <=0.1, not a model confidence probability. Large error is inclusive >=0.3.
All metrics use fixed canonical image identity and floating-point boundary tolerance.

| Output | Numeric pairs | Within 0.1 | MAE | Error >=0.3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Raw Flash v2 | 4,843 | 50.73% | 0.1651 | 22.22% |
| Final continuous (primary evaluation precision) | 4,860 | 63.60% | 0.0865 | 2.16% |
| Final one decimal (selected delivery format) | 4,860 | 84.61% | 0.0761 | 3.60% |

Raw Flash has 17 NA predictions on numeric reference items; the frozen mixture
produces a numeric output for all 4,860. This coverage difference must not be hidden.
On the **same 4,843 raw-numeric items**, the comparisons are:

| Output | Within 0.1 | MAE | Error >=0.3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| flash_raw | 50.73% | 0.1651 | 22.22% |
| final_continuous | 63.58% | 0.0866 | 2.17% |
| final_rounded | 84.58% | 0.0761 | 3.59% |

Rounding was already selected before this validation, but changes tolerance-boundary
decisions: it raises within-0.1 and also raises the >=0.3 error count (105 -> 175).
The continuous hit rate of 63.60% is below the historical >70% aspiration. Report
both precisions; 84.61% rounded agreement is not evidence of 84.61% exact accuracy
or an improvement in visual recognition caused by rounding.

## Distribution and interpretation limits

Across evaluable items pooled within each dimension, reference/model standard
deviations are: safety 0.220/0.186, interface 0.214/0.176, greenery 0.194/0.134,
and maintenance 0.145/0.092 (one-decimal model output). The learned estimates
compress variation, especially greenery and maintenance. Agreement of individual
scores is not a validation of A/B gap detection, ranking or a causal street-design
conclusion. The latter require their own paired and spatial analysis.

The earlier 200-point test report describes the historical base, not this
canonical low-safety extension. It was not rerun to generate a new extension
test score. The repeated 488-point development estimates are also not new holdout
evidence. The accepted team reference is used as supplied; these metrics make no
additional claim about independent annotation provenance.
